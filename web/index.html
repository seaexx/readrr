One book, start to finish 06 steps
  1. 01

    Scan it

    Point your camera at the barcode on the back. The title, author and cover fill in for you, or you can search by title or author instead.

    Barcode · title · author
  2. 02

    List it

    Say whether it's a trade, a loan or a gift, note the condition and add a photo. It shows up for readers within 25 miles.

    Trade · borrow · gift
  3. 03

    Ask for it

    See something you want? Send a request with a short note. The owner accepts or declines from their inbox.

    Requests · inbox
  4. 04

    Talk it through

    Once a request is accepted, a chat opens between the two of you to sort out the details.

    Real-time chat
  5. 05

    Meet somewhere public

    Suggest a café, library, bookshop or community space from real places nearby. When you both agree, the spot stays pinned to the top of the chat.

    Café · library · bookshop
  6. 06

    Close the loop

    You both confirm the swap happened, then rate each other. Ratings show on profiles, so you know who you're meeting next time.

    Confirm · rate
