How we use information

  • Provide the core service: accounts, the feed, book listings, swaps, and chat.
  • Show swaps near you and alert you when a book on your wishlist is listed nearby.
  • Send notifications about swap requests, messages, and meetups.
  • Keep the community safe (handling blocks and reports).
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

How we use location

Location is optional — you can browse without it. If you enable it, we use your device location to calculate distances to nearby swaps and to match books on your wishlist. Other readers never see your exact location; they only ever see an approximate distance (for example, “1.2 mi away”). You can turn location off at any time in your device settings.

How information is shared

  • With other users: your username, profile (city, bio, photo), your posts and listings, your average rating, and the messages you send in a swap are visible to the people you interact with.
  • With service providers: Supabase (hosting, database, authentication, file storage) and Expo (delivery of push notifications). Book details are looked up from Open Library and Google Books, and public meetup venue suggestions come from OpenStreetMap.
  • We never sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (available in the app under Profile), your profile, posts, swaps, and messages are removed. Some records may persist briefly in backups before being overwritten.

Your rights and choices

  • Access & update: edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and associated data from Profile → Delete account.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Location: grant or revoke location access in your device settings.
